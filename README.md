# NUS EE5139 Information Theory — Cheatsheet

Personal cheatsheet for **NUS EE5139 Information Theory**, AY 2026/27 Semester 1, prepared as an examination reference sheet permitted under the course examination rules.

## PDF

[**View / download EE5139_CS.pdf**](EE5139_CS.pdf)

Two A4 pages, designed for double-sided printing. Print at **100% / actual size** and flip on the **long edge**.

## Coverage

- Chapter 0: mathematical foundations and finite fields
- Chapter 1: entropy, mutual information, KL divergence and chain rules
- Chapter 2: source coding, Shannon / Huffman codes, typical sets and Fano's inequality
- Chapter 3: hypothesis testing, total variation distance and error exponents
- Chapter 4: randomness extraction, min-entropy and universal hashing
- Chapter 5: error-correcting codes
- Chapter 6: channel capacity and standard channels
- Chapter 7: noisy channel coding, converse proofs, feedback and AWGN
- Chapter 8: learning theory and sample complexity
- Additional exam methods, Gaussian formulas and proof templates
